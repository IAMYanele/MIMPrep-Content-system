# Thomas reel covers: what was approved, what was rejected

All covers are 1080x1920 Instagram reel covers for @thomas_d_c. Paths are on Yanele's
PC (`F:\thomas b-roll\...`). Open the approved ones before designing a new cover. They
are the reference.

## The look

- **Photo:** a real photo or video frame of Thomas, never regenerated. Tight, confident,
  composed: arms crossed, fist on chin, hands clasped, or a calm look just off camera.
  Mouth closed.
- **Grade:** dark, muted, editorial. The room is pulled down and desaturated, Thomas
  keeps his colour, with a soft vignette and fine grain. On a busy street, one grade
  over the whole frame (no halo). A bright photo stays bright, with near-black ink.
- **Type:** Helvetica Now Text Medium, ALL CAPS, warm cream `#F2ECE2` (near-black
  `#18181A` on bright photos). A small tracked kicker (~50px, +7 tracking) sits above
  a big word or two (auto-sized up to ~320px, -2% tracking). Flat ink: no glow,
  outline, gradient or shadow.
- **Layering:** the big word's bottom edge tucks behind the top of his hair, so the
  hair is in front of the letters, like a magazine masthead.
- **Grid-safe:** all text inside y 240-1680, the centre 3:4 the profile grid shows.

## The pattern behind the feedback

1. **The image wins or loses the cover.** Most rounds were about the photo, not the
   words. Start from the strongest real image, and offer a studio or same-shoot option
   alongside a library photo, because the user's preference flips between them.
2. **Hooks, not titles.** One short kicker plus one punchy big word.
3. **Show, don't list.** Mock options on the real cover. Send sheets, not
   descriptions.
4. **Never silent edits.** If you paint anything out of a frame (a burned-in caption,
   lettering Nano Banana invented on a coffee cup), say so.
