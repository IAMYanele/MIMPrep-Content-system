# MiM Prep cover style: what was approved, what was rejected

## The brand

- **Reference:** Clint's carousel "7 Green Flags of a Strong Business School Applicant". It has a white paper background, a huge royal-blue rounded sans headline, a condensed italic serif line, a blue pill with white italic text, a small blue subtitle, and the MIMPREP logo at the top. The team said: "take exactly this blue... very academic."
- **Colours** (from `brand.ts`):

  | Role | Hex |
  |---|---|
  | Blue (primary) | `#004AAD` |
  | Light blue (second line) | `#4490F6` |
  | Paper | `#F1F1F1` |
  | Dot grid | `#C5CAD6` |

- **Type:**
  - Headline: Poppins SemiBold with a 1px stroke.
  - Kicker and pill: Georgia Pro Condensed Italic.
  - Subtitle: Poppins Medium.

## Rejected, in order, with the team's words (Fathom call 2026-10-19, then chat on 2026-10-20)

1. **Blue sketch collage with AI props** (grad cap, binder clips, passport, globe) and the Montserrat headline.
   - Flavien: "a little too much things on the detail... the graduate cap with the string... looks like classic AI-generated assets."
   - Thomas: "not a big fan of the font."
   - Alex: "has to be maybe a little more modern."
   - The photo-card concept was "already one level above".
2. **Thomas as the subject.** "With the photos of the girls, that would be much better."
3. **The girls inside a rounded photo card.** "I don't like the whole box thing."
4. **Nano Banana swapping the background with the girl in the edit** (library, campus). "Doesn't look realistic... looks like you did something to the face with AI. You're only supposed to cut the person out and then put them in the background." The one exception was Maria's office version, which passed.
5. **A cutout with visible straight arm edges.** "Their arms seem as if they were cut out, and that doesn't really look good." The fix is to run the edges off the canvas or line them up with the arch's sides.
6. **Maria in front of the arch.** It looked off "because of her orientation in relation to the blue shape". The circle replaced it.

## Picking frames

- Use phone UGC clips in the creators' Drive folders.
- Prefer bright, clean takes (Maria's white-wall reels, Manon's Sept 26 white-top set).
- Mathilde's clips are 1080p, so crop less.
- Choose a mouth-closed soft smile with open eyes, at the sharpest frame within a few tenths of a second.
